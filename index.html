What should I notice?

Use these coaching notes to interpret the P&L instead of just watching numbers move.

Gross margin is what remains after direct production or purchase costs. It helps answer whether the core activity is economically attractive before labor and overhead.
EBITDA removes interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization to focus attention on operating earnings. It is useful, but it is not the same thing as cash flow.
Net income is the bottom line after operating expenses, depreciation, interest, and simplified taxes. A profitable company can still have cash pressure because timing and working capital matter.
Teaching model: This simulator is designed to show financial relationships, not to replace ALCIVIA accounting, tax, forecasting, or financial-advice processes. Actual classification and treatment of costs can vary.
